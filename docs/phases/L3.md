# Phase L3 — Checks, clips and the declaration

What it adds:

- **Checks after every upload** (background, one at a time). The checks Claru runs when a clip is sealed, run
  first on our side so a file Claru would refuse is stopped here, with a reason:
  container really MP4 (not a renamed .mov), picture at least 1080 tall, 4:3 (16:9 = warning on this project),
  29.97/30 or 59.94/60 fps (PAL refused), a keyframe at least every second, no gap over 100 ms, no rotation tag,
  no HDR, no location, no artist/author tag, at least 5 minutes long; audio missing = warning.
  The whole file is also read through once (no full decode): a truncated or damaged file is reported unreadable.
  FROZEN and PADDED_FRAME_RATE are Claru's own statistics and are left to Claru.
- **Clips (even split).** Up to 30 minutes: one clip, a hard link to the original (no extra disk). Longer: the
  fewest clips that fit 5–30 minutes, cut into equal parts on keyframes with a stream copy (never re-encoded),
  keeping video, audio and a GoPro telemetry track. Nothing is dropped; 33 min → 2 × 16.5 min.
  Each clip gets its Claru reference: `cvu-<recording id>-c01`, `-c02`, …
- **Declaration page.** Choose recordings; fill category (from Claru's live list), country, site id, camera,
  mount once; then per recording the start date and time and the four consents (never pre-ticked; the key
  names are shown exactly as Claru words them). collectorId = the uploader. All four consents must be true.
- **Recordings page:** checks, clips and declaration per recording; Details shows every finding, the clips
  and the declaration; Check again; Delete removes clips too. "Ready for Claru" = checks passed, clips cut,
  declared. Delivery itself is phase L4.
- Background jobs survive a restart (a job interrupted by a stop is re-queued at start).
- Recordings received before L3 are checked automatically at the first start.

Settings (.env): `CLARU_PROJECT_ID`, `CLIP_MIN_SECONDS` (300), `CLIP_MAX_SECONDS` (1800), `CAPTURE_ASPECT` (4_3),
`FFMPEG_PATH`, `FFPROBE_PATH`. The server warns at start if the clip rules differ from Claru's.

Gate:

- `npm run check` passes (real MP4s are generated for the tests: a good one cut into three clips, a 16:9 one,
  a deliberately bad one with eight faults, a truncated one).
- With your real recordings: one under 30 minutes → 1 clip; one over 30 minutes → equal clips that each play
  in QuickTime from the right moment; a phone video or a .mov renamed to .mp4 → failed with reasons.
- Declare two recordings in one go; both show "Ready for Claru".
